I’ve been working on bringing one of Arma 3’s most iconic experiences"King of the Hill" into DayZ.

This mod completely reimagines how DayZ is played. Instead of looting and surviving, players join one of two factions West (Blue) or East (Red) and battle for control over key mission zones scattered across Chernarus. The goal is simple: capture and hold the hill to earn points and win the round.

Here’s what’s inside right now:

Two fully functional teams with spawn bases and safe zones

Around 10 rotating zones across Chernarus

A priority area inside the main zone that gives bonus points

XP and Money systems that reward kills, assists, captures, and team play

A persistent leveling system that saves your progress

Custom Weapon, Clothing, and Vehicle Rental shops

A scoreboard that tracks player stats and performance

No looting from the ground. everything runs through the shop and progression systems

Expansion AI compatibility, so servers can choose to fill empty matches with AI

Optimized code and zone handling for smooth performance even on full servers

A zone voting system that lets players choose the next area after each round


AI Support:
- 10 AIs from each faction will spawn and fight for the zone
- Random AI attacks priority zone
- AI send reinforcements (Max 10 AIs per faction) when other team is winning


Planned Features: (Coming soon after testing)

More unique and challenging zones
Custom faction weapons, uniforms, and vehicles
Spawn-on-radio-operator system
Spawn-on-Spawn-Vehicles system
